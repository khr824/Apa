Citations and page numbering

Check these examples on how to create in-text citations, references, and how to number pages APA style.

  • In-text: Place citations at the bottom-right of the slide or adjacent to the cited point.
  • Reference Section: Terminal slides must list all cited works with a hanging indent.
  • Page Numbering: Integers should be placed consistently in the bottom-right quadrant.

Layout (spacing)

Formal documents require clear spacing and navigation structure.

  • Spacing: Double-space all body text and reference lists.
  • Indents: Use 0.5 in. indents for paragraphs and hanging indents for references.
  • Table of Contents: Required for underground theses; lists all major section headers and sub-headers.
  • Page Breaks: Forced breaks before the Title Page, Abstract, and References.

How to cite when the source has grammar errors [sic]

Use [sic] in italics within brackets to indicate an error (spelling or grammar) exists in the original alternative source.

"The collective remains commited [sic] to total decentralization."
