📝 Thesis Layout Basics

  • Margins: 1 inch (2.54 cm) structural borders standard on all top, bottom, left, and right bounds.
  • Typography: Readable choices include 11-pt Calibri, 11-pt Arial, or 12-pt Times New Roman.
  • Line Spacing: Strict double-spacing completely throughout the entire academic manuscript.
  • Page Numbers: Placed at top-right corner, starting immediately with page 1 on the title page.

🔗 In-Text Citations

Parenthetical Format

Include author and year separated by a comma inside parentheses at the end of a clause.

Structural linguistics defines pedagogy as a dynamic system (Smith, 2023).

Narrative Format

Weave the author's name smoothly into your active sentence structure, instantly following with the publication year.

Smith (2023) argued that structural linguistics defines pedagogy...

📚 Reference Essentials

All reference list items adhere to a hanging indent of 0.5 inches and split into four pillars:

  • Author: Who produced it? (e.g., Early, J. M.)
  • Date: When was it published? (e.g., 2026)
  • Title: What is the resource named? (Italicized)
  • Source: Where can it be retrieved? (e.g., Publisher or DOI)
